Page 15 · Q(b)

Student: working incomplete

Correct: x = 2L/(5y+22)

目标：先求两个图形面积，再把 x 变成公式的主项。
Step 1. Rectangle: length = 2x, width = y+5.
Area = 2x(y+5)=2xy+10x
Step 2. Triangle: base = y+4, height = 6x.
Area = 1/2(y+4)(6x)=3x(y+4)=3xy+12x
Step 3. Total area is 2L.
2L=(2xy+10x)+(3xy+12x)=5xy+22x
Step 4. Factorise x.
2L=x(5y+22)
Step 5. Divide both sides by 5y+22.
x=2L/(5y+22)
Check: x is alone and the answer contains only L and y.

Practice 9 · Speed & Acceleration

Section A · Q9

Student: 70.59 km/h

Correct: 75 km/h

Step 1. Outward journey: distance 300 km, speed 80 km/h.
t₁=300÷80=3.75 h
Step 2. Return trip took 30 min =0.5 h longer.
t₂=3.75+0.5=4.25 h

300+300=600 km
