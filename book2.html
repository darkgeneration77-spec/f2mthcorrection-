Earlier Practice

Page 15 · Q(b)

Student: incomplete

Correct: x = 2L / (5y + 22)

1. Rectangle area = 2x(y+5)=2xy+10x.
2. Triangle area = 1/2(y+4)(6x)=3xy+12x.
3. Total area: 2L=5xy+22x.
4. Factorise: 2L=x(5y+22).
x = 2L/(5y+22)

Polygon Practice · Q2(a)

Student: 84°

Correct: 96°

Decagon central angle = 360°÷10=36°.
Hexagon central angle = 360°÷6=60°.
∠QOW = 36°+60° = 96°
不要错误使用 180°−36°−60°。

Circle Practice · Q15

Student: 39.6 cm

Correct: 136.4 cm

Major angle = 360°−81°=279°.
Arc = 279/360 × 2 × 22/7 × 28 = 136.4 cm
39.6 cm 是 minor arc，不是 major arc。
