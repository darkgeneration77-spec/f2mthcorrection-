Page 15 · Q(b) · Algebraic Formula

Question condition: A composite figure consists of a rectangle of dimensions 2x by (y+5) and a triangle with base (y+4), height 6x. The total area is 2L. Make x the subject.

Student: incomplete

Correct: x=2L/(5y+22)

Why wrong: The areas were not fully combined and x was not isolated.
Key concept: Total area = sum of component areas; then factorise the required subject.
Step 1 Understand: Find rectangle and triangle separately.
Rectangle=2x(y+5)=2xy+10x
Triangle=½(y+4)(6x)=3xy+12x
Step 2 Combine:
2L=2xy+10x+3xy+12x=5xy+22x
Step 3 Factorise and isolate x:
2L=x(5y+22) ⇒ x=2L/(5y+22)
Final Answer: x=2L/(5y+22)
Check: x is alone; RHS contains only L and y.
Exam Answer
2L=2x(y+5)+½(y+4)(6x)
2L=5xy+22x
2L=x(5y+22)
x=2L/(5y+22)

Polygon Practice · Q2(a)

Question condition: A regular decagon and regular hexagon meet at centre O. Find ∠QOW formed by their adjacent central angles.

Student: 84°

Correct: 96°

84° came from using a subtraction relationship that the diagram does not provide.
Central angle of a regular n-gon = 360°÷n.
Decagon: 360°÷10=36°.
Hexagon: 360°÷6=60°.
The two angles are adjacent, so add them.
∠QOW=36°+60°=96°
Final Answer: 96°
36+60=96 and both are parts of the required angle.
Exam Answer
360°÷10=36°
360°÷6=60°
∠QOW=36°+60°=96°

Practice 8 · Functions

Section A · Q1 · Relation Type

Question condition: In the mapping, 5 maps to 15 and 20, while 20 is also an output of more than one input. Classify the relation.

Student: one-to-many

Correct: many-to-many

One-to-many notices only repeated outputs from one input; here both repetition directions occur.
Check two things: Does one input have multiple outputs? Does one output receive multiple inputs?
5→15 and 5→20: one input has many outputs.
20 receives more than one input: many inputs share an output.
Final Answer: Many-to-many
Exam Answer
Many-to-many relation.

Final Answer: D
Repeated outputs are allowed; repeated input with different outputs is not.

D, because input 2 maps to two outputs, 8 and 10.

Practice 9 · Speed & Acceleration

Section A · Q9 · Average Speed

Question condition: Hafizi travels 300 km from Kemaman to Bachok at 80 km/h. The return trip takes 30 minutes longer. Find the average speed for the whole journey.

Student: 70.59 km/h

Correct: 75 km/h

Average speed is total distance÷total time, not the simple average of two speeds.
Average speed=total distance/total time.
Outbound time=300÷80=3.75 h.
30 min=0.5 h, so return time=4.25 h.
Total distance=600 km; total time=8 h.
Average speed=600÷8=75 km/h
Final Answer: 75 km/h
600 km at average 75 km/h takes exactly 8 h.
Exam Answer
t₁=300/80=3.75 h
t₂=3.75+0.5=4.25 h
Average speed=(300+300)/(3.75+4.25)
=75 km/h

Question condition: A vehicle accelerates from rest to 96 km/h in 8 s. Find acceleration in km/s².

Equivalent value is 3.33 m/s².

a=(v−u)/t

Section C · Q2(a)(i) · Deceleration

Question condition: During the final 8 s, an object decelerates uniformly at 1.5 m/s² and comes to rest. Find its speed at the start of those 8 s.

−12=−u ⇒ u=12 m/s

12−1.5(8)=0 m/s, so it stops exactly after 8 s.
