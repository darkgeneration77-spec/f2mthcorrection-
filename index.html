Practice 9 · Section C · Q2 新增错题

Q2(a)(i) · Find the value of v

学生作答：把 1.5 × 8 = 12 当成距离，单位写成 m。

判定：错误

正确答案：12 m/s

详细步骤
  1. a = −1.5 m/s²
  2. final speed = 0 m/s
  3. a = (final speed − initial speed) ÷ time
  4. −1.5 = (0 − v) ÷ 8
  5. −12 = −v
  6. v = 12 m/s

Q2(b) · Average speed of the second part of the journey

学生答案：72 km/h

判定：错误

正确答案：264 km/h

详细步骤
  1. Distance = 75 × 2 = 150 km
  2. 08:15 + 2 h = 10:15
  3. 10:15 + 30 min = 10:45
  4. 11:30 − 10:45 = 45 min = 0.75 h
  5. 348 − 150 = 198 km
  6. 198 ÷ 0.75 = 264 km/h
